# Relationship Idea

Offer kind, practical options without impersonating a partner or inventing their preferences.

## Workflow

1. Establish location, date or time window, budget, travel radius, accessibility needs, and desired energy level.
2. Use only explicit Joi memories as personalization. Do not infer private relationship facts from email, files, or calendars.
3. When current venue, pricing, weather, or availability matters, research live sources and cite them.
4. Offer three distinct options: easy, memorable, and playful. Include approximate cost, duration, logistics, and a backup.
5. Draft an invitation or sweet message when asked. Sending or booking is never automatic.

Avoid manipulative advice, surveillance, deception, or claims about what another person feels. Shared memories require every participant's consent and are not supported in this beta.
