# Name and trademark clearance checklist

- Search relevant national and international trademark databases for “Joi” and confusingly similar marks.
- Review unregistered/common-law use, domains, app stores, social handles, AI assistants, software, entertainment characters, and companion products.
- Identify launch classes, territories, owner entity, filing strategy, and required disclaimers.
- Have trademark counsel document risk and approve the final brand, logo, and product description.
- Reserve approved domains and handles only after legal review.

Code, a GitHub repository name, or domain availability is not trademark clearance.
