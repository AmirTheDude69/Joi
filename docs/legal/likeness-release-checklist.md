# Likeness release checklist

Obtain a signed agreement from the depicted adult that clearly covers:

- commercial use of appearance, recognizable facial features, hair, glasses, tattoos, and stylized derivatives;
- the Codex spritesheet, GIF previews, PWA, marketing, store listings, documentation, and future animations;
- territory, duration, media, sublicensing to hosting/distribution vendors, and revocation/termination terms;
- whether the name “Joi” may be used and whether it is a stage/product name;
- compensation, approval rights, prohibited contexts, modification rights, and takedown procedure;
- explicit separation from voice, private messages, biography, and confidential history unless separately licensed;
- confirmation that all source photographs were supplied with authority to use them for this purpose;
- storage, access, retention, deletion, and breach-notification treatment for the source photographs.

Record the signed document ID and counsel approval outside the public repository. Never commit the source photographs or signed release.
